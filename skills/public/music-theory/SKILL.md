# Music theory

When an answer needs the actual notes of a chord, arpeggio, scale or mode, or
the chords that belong to a key, get them from the tool first:

ACTION: chord | Cmaj7
ACTION: chord | Dm7 G7 Cmaj7
ACTION: chord | F# harmonic minor scale
ACTION: chord | chords in Eb major

Sharps and flats are easy to get wrong from memory (G# major has an F##,
Gb major a Cb), and the tool spells them by letter the way a teacher would.
Use its notes exactly, then explain: which degree each note is, why the chord
or scale sounds the way it does, and a fingering or voicing tip if it's for an
instrument Sarvin plays.
